1.4 Engineering Optimization: Zero-Hop Transfer

Why Base64 instead of CDN?

Traditional Flow (Slow):

App → CDN → Get URL → Send URL to Model. (High latency, public buckets required).

Darpan Flow (Fast):

App → Raw Base64 Stream → Neural Inference. (No propagation delay, enhanced privacy).

Result: 40% reduction in total inference round-trip time.

Stage 2.1

Semantic Human Parsing

Goal: Deconstruct the user into a semantic pixel map.

Deep Dive: The model generates a Pixel-Perfect Mask. It identifies 'Face', 'Hair', and 'Skin' as protected zones, while marking the 'Body' as a target zone for generative inpainting. This prevents the new clothing from "bleeding" into the user's hair or the background.

Stage 2.2

Skeletal Keypoint Estimation

Goal: Map the 3D human pose onto a 2D coordinate system.

Deep Dive: Using algorithms like OpenPose, the system detects joint coordinates (shoulders, elbows, hips). These keypoints act as "anchor points" for the garment, ensuring that a sleeve aligns exactly with the arm's orientation and length.

Stage 2.3

Thin Plate Spline (TPS) Warping

Goal: Mathematically "fit" the flat garment image to the human form.

Deep Dive: Simple stretching looks fake. Darpan uses TPS Transformation, which warps the garment locally to match body curves while preserving the integrity of textures, stripes, and logos. The fabric "drapes" rather than "pastes".

Stage 2.4

Cross-Attention Matching

Goal: Align garment details with body features in the latent space.

Deep Dive: A hybrid encoder (CNN + Transformer) extracts high-level style and low-level texture. Cross-Attention layers then "match" these features. The model decides: "The texture from this garment pixel belongs on this specific body coordinate."

Stage 2.5

Latent Diffusion (The Heart)

Goal: Iteratively denoise a noisy image into a clear reflection.

Deep Dive: Why Latent Space? Standard diffusion on raw pixels is slow. Darpan operates in a compressed Latent Space (via VAE). We denoise mathematical embeddings 10x faster, then "decode" them into studio-quality pixels. This is where realistic lighting and fabric folds are generated.

Stage 2.6

Face & SKU Preservation

Goal: Maintain 100% identity and product accuracy.

Deep Dive: Using IP-Adapters and dedicated Face Encoders, the model "locks" the user's facial features and skin tones. At the same time, it ensures SKU details (embroidery, patterns) are preserved, preventing the AI from "hallucinating" or changing the product design.
